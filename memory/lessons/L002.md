# L002 Make 3D Space Legible Through Camera And Depth

## Tags

science, spatial-relationship, camera-motion, animation-design-error

## Error Type

animation-design-error

## Error Manifestation

A 3D-style animation had camera movement, but the object scale, depth order, and motion direction did not clearly reveal the spatial relationship. Viewers could not tell what the camera movement was explaining.

## Correct Approach

Plan the camera path as part of the knowledge expression: use push-in for introduction, orbit for spatial relationship, top/side angle for structure, and controlled holds for comprehension. Tie depth changes to the concept being explained.

## Reusable Rule

Camera motion must explain space, not merely move; every camera move needs a specific knowledge purpose.

## Applicable Scenarios

Molecular structure, astronomy, geography flythroughs, product exploded views, mechanical structure, and any animation requiring 3D spatial understanding.
